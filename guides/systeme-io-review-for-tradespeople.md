## What Systeme.io is, in one line

Systeme.io is an all-in-one online tool for small businesses. It combines simple web pages, email lists, automatic emails, booking calendars and online courses in one account. "All-in-one" simply means you log in to one place instead of paying for separate tools that need connecting.

It is a marketing tool, not job software. Its pricing page does not list quotes, job sheets, invoices or accounts, so keep using your job management or accounting software for those.

## What the free plan includes

These figures come from Systeme.io's own [pricing page](https://systeme.io/pricing), checked on 25 November 2026. Plans change, so check the page before you sign up.

- **Up to 2,000 contacts.** A contact is one person on your list, such as a customer who asked for a quote.
- **3 sales funnels.** A funnel is a short set of web pages, such as a quote request page and its thank-you page.
- **1 email campaign.** A campaign is a set of emails sent in order on a timer, such as three quote follow-ups.
- **1 automation rule.** An automation rule is an "if this happens, do that" instruction, such as "when someone fills in the quote form, start the follow-up emails".
- **Unlimited emails** to your contacts.
- **1 custom domain**, so pages can sit on your own web address.
- **1 blog and 1 course.**
- **No transaction fees** from Systeme.io when you take payments through Stripe or PayPal (those providers still charge their own fees).
- **A booking calendar.** Systeme.io's booking calendar page says it is included on the free plan.

The free plan does not expire and does not need a card to sign up.

## What the free plan can't do

This is where an honest review matters.

1. **Only one email sequence.** If you use your one campaign for quote follow-ups, you cannot also run a separate sequence for, say, after-job review requests. You would need a paid plan or a second tool.
2. **Only one automation rule.** Anything beyond one "if this, then that" step is manual on the free plan. For example, removing a customer from the follow-up emails when they book is a quick manual step.
3. **Booking reminders are emails, not texts.** The reminder wording is written by the system and cannot be edited, and you can have up to three reminders per event. If most of your customers only read text messages, a booking tool with SMS reminders may suit you better.
4. **It does not replace job or accounting software.** No job scheduling for a team, no quotes with line items, no tax returns.
5. **Contact limit of 2,000.** Plenty for most local trades, but a busy business with years of past customers may reach it.

If you need more than one sequence or rule, the paid plans listed on the pricing page start with Startup at $17 a month (5,000 contacts, 10 funnels), then Webinar at $47 and Unlimited at $97, before any local tax. You can cancel at any time and the account drops back to the free plan.

## Who it suits, and who should skip it

**Good fit:** a sole trader or small team with no email list, no follow-up system and no budget for several tools. If customers go quiet after quotes, or forget appointments, the free plan can fix one of those this week.

**Probably not a fit:** a business that needs text-message reminders, team job scheduling, or a system connected to quoting or accounting software. Salon-specific booking software or your job management app may already do what you need.

## FAQ

### Is Systeme.io really free?

Yes. The free plan never expires and needs no card. It is limited to 2,000 contacts, 3 funnels, 1 email campaign and 1 automation rule, so it suits one simple system rather than a whole business.

### Is Systeme.io good for beginners?

It is designed for people who do not code: pages, emails and bookings are set up with menus and drag-and-drop editors. Expect an afternoon for your first system, and test it with your own email address before sharing it.

### Can Systeme.io send text message reminders?

The booking reminders we cover are emails only. Systeme.io's pricing page lists SMS marketing, but it does not show which plans include it, so check with Systeme.io before relying on texts.
