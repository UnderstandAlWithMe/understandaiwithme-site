Most business owners know admin eats their week. Far fewer can say which tasks eat it. Without that list, it is hard to choose the right fix: a template, a tool, an automation or a person.

A one-week time audit gives you the list. The logging is the only part that needs discipline. An AI assistant such as ChatGPT or Claude can do the sorting, the maths and the first draft of your plan.

## Why a week of notes beats a guess

Our memory of where time goes is unreliable. Big, annoying tasks feel huge, while small repeated ones (re-typing an address, finding an old email, copying numbers between systems) quietly add up. A short log shows both, and it gives you a real number to judge any tool or hire against.

## Step 1: Log every admin task for five working days

Keep it rough. You are looking for patterns, not timesheet precision.

1. Pick one place to log: a note on your phone, a spreadsheet or a paper notebook.
2. Each time you finish an admin task, write one line: **what it was, roughly how long it took, and what triggered it** (a customer email, a deadline, a colleague asking).
3. Round to the nearest 5 minutes. "Chased 2 invoices, 15 min, Friday habit" is perfect.
4. If you forget during the day, spend two minutes at the end of the day writing down what you remember.

If typing is a chore, record a short voice note each evening instead and turn it into text with a transcription tool. A tool like HappyScribe on our [recommended tools](https://understandaiwithme.com/recommended-tools/) page can do this, as can the voice input in most AI apps.

## Step 2: Remove anything sensitive before you paste it

Before you hand the log to AI, take out customer names, staff details, amounts and anything confidential. Replace them with labels such as "Customer A" or "supplier". The AI does not need the details to spot patterns, and keeping private data out of AI tools is a good habit in general.

## Step 3: Ask AI to sort and total it

Paste the whole log into ChatGPT or Claude with a clear instruction. This prompt works well:

"Here is a log of my admin tasks for one week. Group them into categories (for example: invoicing, email, scheduling, bookkeeping, customer updates). For each category give the total time, how many times it happened, and what usually triggered it. Show the result as a table, sorted by total time. Do not invent tasks that are not in the log."

Check the totals against your log. AI can make arithmetic mistakes, so add up the top two or three categories yourself.

## Step 4: Ask what to fix first

Now ask for a plan, using your own table as the input:

"For each category, suggest the simplest fix in this order of preference: stop doing it, use a template, use a feature in a tool I probably already have, automate it, or hand it to someone else. Rank the categories by time saved for the least effort. Flag anything where you need more information from me."

You should get a short list that looks something like:

- **Repeated emails:** saved templates or AI drafts.
- **Chasing payments:** automatic invoice reminders.
- **Receipts:** capture them at the point of purchase.
- **Weekly reporting:** one AI prompt with a fixed format.
- **Bookkeeping catch-up:** a freelance bookkeeper for a few hours a month.

Treat the suggestions as a starting point, not a verdict. You know your customers and constraints; the AI only knows your log.

## Step 5: Pick one fix and measure it

Choose the top item and fix only that for the next two weeks. Then log that category again for a few days and compare the time with your first week. If it dropped, move on to the next item. If it did not, ask AI why the fix might not be working, giving it both logs.

### When the answer is "get help"

Some categories will not shrink with a template. If one task is taking several hours a week and follows clear steps, it may be worth a small custom automation or a freelancer. Your log is now the brief: it shows what the task is, how often it happens and how long it takes.

## Common mistakes

- **Logging only the big tasks.** The small, frequent ones are often where the easy wins are.
- **Auditing a weird week.** Avoid holiday weeks or your busiest week of the year.
- **Trying to fix everything at once.** One fix, measured, beats five half-finished ones.
- **Trusting the totals blindly.** Always check the AI's maths.

## The short version

- Log every admin task for five working days: what, how long, what triggered it.
- Remove names, amounts and private details before pasting into AI.
- Ask ChatGPT or Claude to group, total and rank the tasks in a table.
- Ask for the simplest fix for each category, then check its reasoning.
- Fix one thing, re-measure, then move on to the next.
