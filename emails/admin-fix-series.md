Subject: The one-week admin audit
Preview: A notepad and five minutes a day

Hi {first_name},

Before fixing anything, it helps to know where the time actually goes. Most people guess wrong.

Try this for one week:

1. Every time you do an admin task, jot it down with how long it took. A notes app is fine.
2. At the end of the week, circle anything you did more than twice.
3. Put a star next to anything that follows the same steps every time.

The circled-and-starred tasks are where better tools or AI pay off first. Everything else can wait.

## Email 3 (2 days after email 2)

Subject: Is your business actually ready for AI?
Preview: The honest answer is often "not yet", and that's fine

Hi {first_name},

AI is brilliant at tasks that are repetitive, written down and already digital. It's much less useful for work that changes every time or lives in someone's head.

So before buying anything with "AI" in the name, ask:

- Does this task follow the same steps most of the time?
- Could I write those steps on one page?
- Is the information already in a digital tool, not on paper?

Three yeses means it's a good AI candidate. Any no means a simple tool or a checklist will probably save you more time right now.

I built a quick quiz that scores this for you and suggests one next step:

Subject: Get AI to write your emails (in your voice)
Preview: A 20-minute setup that keeps paying back

Hi {first_name},

If you send the same kinds of emails every week (quotes, follow-ups, "thanks for your enquiry"), this is the quickest AI win there is.

The short version:

1. Find five emails you've sent that you're happy with.
2. Write a few lines on how you sound and what you always include.
3. Save both as a Project in Claude or a custom GPT in ChatGPT.
4. From then on, one line from you ("quote follow-up, 5 days, no reply") gets you a draft in your voice.
