Practical AI for real work. No coding, no hype.

UnderstandAIwithMe helps small businesses and busy professionals get hours back from admin, using AI where it genuinely helps and better tools or people where it doesn’t.

Hi, I’m Constantin

I build AI workflows and explain them in plain English on YouTube and TikTok. Most of the people I talk to don’t need an “AI strategy”. They need the invoice reminders to go out on time, payroll to stop eating a day, and fewer copy-and-paste jobs.

This site is where the videos turn into step-by-step guides, plus a short list of tools and partners I’d actually use.

How this site works

  1. Pick the problem that costs you most time. Each one has a page with three things to do first.
  2. Where a tool or partner is the fastest fix, we name one. Some of those links are affiliate links.
  3. For bigger jobs, you can describe a task and a vetted AI automation partner replies with a rough estimate.

Our rules

  • Useful first. Every guide must contain steps you can do today, even if you never click a link.
  • One recommendation per problem. Not a list of twenty. If we wouldn’t use it, it isn’t here.
  • Clear about money. We may earn a commission when you sign up through a link. It never changes your price. See the disclosure.
  • Not financial advice. For finance products, always check the provider’s own terms and costs.
