How to Turn Customer Calls Into Notes and a Follow-Up Email With AI

By Constantin · 2026-11-27 · 5 min read

Prefer video?

A customer rings about a regular clean. Three bedrooms, a dog, a key under the mat, every other Thursday, and could you do the oven once a month? You say "no problem", hang up, and two hours later half of it has gone. This guide shows you how to turn customer calls into notes and a ready-to-send follow-up email with AI, so the quote is right first time and the customer can see you were listening.

We use a cleaning business as the example, but the same five steps work for any trade where customers explain the job on the phone.

What you will build

  • A recording of the call, made only with the customer's permission.
  • A transcript and AI notes. A transcript is simply the call written out word for word.
  • A job checklist that pulls out the details you need to quote, with question marks on anything unclear.
  • A follow-up email in your own voice that repeats back what the customer asked for.
  • A privacy check before anything goes near an AI chat.

You need a phone or computer, a transcription tool and a free AI assistant such as ChatGPT or Claude.

How to turn customer calls into notes in five steps

Step 1: Record the call, with permission

At the start of the call, say something like: "Do you mind if I record this so I don't miss anything? I'll only use it to write up your quote." Only record if they say yes.

Rules on recording calls differ between countries, and in some countries between states, so check what applies where you work before you start. In the UK the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) publishes guidance on handling personal data; in the US and Australia, check your state's rules as well as national ones.

How to record:

  1. Video calls (Zoom, Google Meet, Teams): use the platform's own record button, or an AI notetaker that joins the call.
  2. Phone calls: use your phone's built-in call recorder if it has one, or put the customer on speaker and record with a voice memo app on a second device.
  3. In person (a home visit to look at the job): a voice memo app on your phone works the same way.

Step 2: Turn the recording into a transcript and notes

Upload the recording to a transcription tool. In our video we use HappyScribe, which transcribes audio and video files in more than 150 languages and can produce AI summaries of the conversation. Its free plan includes 10 minutes of file transcription, enough to test the whole system on a short practice call. Test on a practice call first, with a friend playing the customer. Never test on a real customer's call.

When the transcript comes back, skim it. Transcription is good but not perfect: names, street names and numbers are where mistakes usually hide.

Step 3: Turn the notes into a job checklist

Copy the transcript or notes into ChatGPT or Claude and paste this prompt:

"These are notes from a call with a new cleaning customer. List: the rooms, how often, the preferred day, access instructions, pets, extra jobs, anything they want avoided, and anything I promised to send. Mark anything unclear with a question mark."

Change the list to fit your trade. A plumber might ask for the problem, access, parking and when the customer is in.

The question marks are the most useful part. They are the things to confirm before you quote, instead of guessing.

Step 4: Write the follow-up email in your voice

If you have already written a short "how we write" note (see our guide to AI emails in your own voice), paste it in first. Then ask:

"Using this checklist, write a short follow-up email. Thank them for the call, repeat back what they asked for in a few bullet points, ask about anything marked with a question mark, and end with my booking link."

Before you send it:

  1. Check every bullet matches something the customer actually said.
  2. Set the price yourself. AI should never decide what you charge.
  3. Read it out loud. If it sounds like a robot, ask the AI to make it "plainer" or "more like a text message".

Repeating the customer's own words back is what makes the email feel personal. If you take bookings online, the end of this email is the best place for your link: our salon booking system guide shows how to set one up. If the customer goes quiet after you quote, a quote follow-up system sends polite reminders for you.

Step 5: The privacy check most people skip

Before you paste anything into an AI chat, delete the sensitive parts:

  • Key safe codes, alarm codes and where the spare key is kept.
  • Anything about health, money or family situations.
  • Full addresses and phone numbers, if the AI does not need them to write the email.

Afterwards:

  1. Delete recordings and transcripts you no longer need.
  2. Keep access details somewhere private, not in an AI chat or a shared document.
  3. Check your AI tool's data settings, including whether your chats can be used to train its models.

FAQ

Is it legal to record customer phone calls?

Often yes, but it depends on where you and the customer are. Some places only need one person on the call to agree; others need everyone to agree. The simplest safe habit is to ask at the start of every call and only record if the customer says yes. Check your local rules before you start.

What should call notes include?

Who called and how to reach them, what they want done, how often, when it suits them, access and any pets, extras they mentioned, anything they want avoided and anything you promised to send. Mark anything you are unsure about so you confirm it before quoting.

Can I use AI without recording the call?

Yes. Jot down rough notes during or straight after the call, even in shorthand, and paste them into the step 3 prompt. The checklist and email still work; you just have less detail to start from.

The short version

  • Ask permission, then record the call (or take quick notes if they say no).
  • A transcription tool turns the recording into text and AI notes.
  • One prompt turns the notes into a job checklist with question marks on anything unclear.
  • A second prompt writes a follow-up email that repeats back what the customer said. You set the price.
  • Strip out codes and private details before pasting anything into AI, and delete recordings you don't need.
